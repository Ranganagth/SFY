QUICK GUIDE · AVAILABLE OFFLINE AFTER FIRST LOAD

If someone is in danger

Call your local emergency number. Do not enter the water to attempt a rescue unless you are trained and equipped. From a safe place, alert a lifeguard or nearby responders and follow their instructions.

Everyday water safety tips
  • Choose designated swimming areas and follow local signs.
  • Wear an appropriate life jacket when boating or fishing near deep water.
  • Never drive or walk through floodwater.
  • Keep children close and actively supervised around water.
Guidance is general. Follow local emergency instructions.
